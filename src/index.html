Four reasons

Four reasons

  1. It doesn't crack as fast.

    Cracks on a pickleball almost always start at the edge of a hole. The LT Pro 48 chamfers every hole edge to spread that stress. Fewer dead balls mid-game, fewer balls in the trash.

  2. The bounce is true.

    48 symmetrically spaced holes and two-piece injection molding give a uniform bounce of about 85 cm. No skips, no dead spots, no "that one was the ball's fault."

  3. It survives our heat.

    Balls soften and slow down in South Florida sun. The LT plays faster with a higher bounce, so a hot afternoon ball still plays like a ball.

  4. It's what the pros play.

    PPA Tour and MLP both use it. If you enter a PPA event, this is the ball you'll be handed. Practice on what you'll compete on.

Fair questions

Fair questions

"The X-40 is the official USAP ball."

True. USA Pickleball sanctions with it. But the two biggest pro tours looked at the whole field and picked the LT.

"It plays too fast for me."

It is quicker off the paddle. Give it two sessions. Your resets and hands speed get sharper, not worse.

"It costs more per ball."

A cracked ball costs the whole group a game. Cost per hour of good play is what actually matters.
